## Two Book Reviews Published in This Month’s C Vu Magazine

The current issue of C Vu (Volume 21, Issue 6 - one of the journals published by the [ACCU](http://accu.org)) has two book reviews from yours truly. The first is for the 3rd edition of [UML Distilled by Martin Fowler](http://www.amazon.co.uk/UML-Distilled-Standard-Addison-Wesley-Technology/dp/0321193687), and the other is for [The Old New Thing by Raymond Chen](http://www.amazon.co.uk/Old-New-Thing-Development-Throughout/dp/0321440307). Neither book is exactly new and the latter I read some years ago, but I noticed a couple of gaps in the [book reviews](http://accu.org/index.php/book_reviews) on the ACCU website (which are searchable by non-members as well as members) and thought I’d fill them in. Both books get a big thumbs up from me*. Of course if you want to read the reviews you’ll either need to [subscribe](http://accu.org/index.php/joining) to the ACCU so that you can download back issues of [C Vu and Overload](http://accu.org/index.php/aboutus/aboutjournals), or wait for them to be published on the website.

*Given the name of this blog and the contents of [my very first post](http://chrisoldwood.blogspot.com/2009/04/apology-to-raymond-chen.html) I don’t think this will be much of a surprise to anyone.
